# safe-by-design

> **Status: concept, and parked. There is no code in this repository yet.**
> It is published for the idea, not for use. Our working code is in
> [harm-signal-api](https://github.com/The-Cocoon-Collective/harm-signal-api).

A game framework and curriculum for teaching digital literacy — *Oregon Trail energy, but
the wagon is your privacy settings.*

## Who it is for

**Older children than our first platform serves.** We have deliberately not put a band on it:
where that band sits is a question for the people who study these children, not one to settle
in a README. That uncertainty is exactly why this is parked rather than in progress.

Our current work is for six- and seven-year-olds, and a six-year-old does not need a curriculum
about password hygiene — they need a place where the risk is not present in the first place.
Digital literacy becomes the right tool at about the age a child starts going places an
adult has not already made safe. That is a different platform, and it is one we intend to
build separately rather than as a wing of the first. Stretching one platform across too many
years is the thing no amount of moderation fixes.

## The idea, when it is time

- A browser-based game engine that works in a classroom and at a kitchen table
- Curriculum modules: privacy, who to trust, passwords, consent, and what a platform does
  with what you give it
- A teacher and parent view showing progress, not behaviour
- Fully offline-capable, because a digital-literacy tool that phones home is an argument
  against itself

## What would help

If you teach this age group, the most useful thing you can tell us is what already exists and
works — we would rather adopt it than duplicate it. Open an issue.
